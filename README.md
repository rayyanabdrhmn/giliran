&#x20;  # Giliran



&#x20;  Fair ticketing for high-demand events.



&#x20;  ## What it is

&#x20;  Giliran is a ticketing platform for high-demand events, like big concerts, where thousands of people try to buy at the same moment. Organisers set up events and seats; buyers wait in a fair queue, hold their seats for a few minutes and pay. It is designed so that no seat can ever be sold twice, even under heavy load.



&#x20;  ## The problem

&#x20;  In 2025, the ticket website and app for My Chemical Romance's Kuala Lumpur concert crashed as fans rushed to buy, and within hours scalpers were reselling tickets at higher prices. Many fans who queued still went home without tickets. It is technically hard because thousands of buyers compete for a limited number of seats at the same moment. The system has to stay fair and never sell one seat twice, even when it is overloaded or a payment fails halfway.
